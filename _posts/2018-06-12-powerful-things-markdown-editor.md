This was my first, so as to speak, 'project' in IIT Bombay, and a pretty hectic one too. XLR8 is (I believe) the flagship event of the Electronics and Robotics Club, and serves as a fantastic introduction to working with electronic components, competing and collaborating with other students. 

In the 2025 edition of XLR8, we were tasked with building a line-following bot that could avoid and withstand obstacles. For my entire team and I, this was our first introduction to working with electronics components, so that along with settling in to our new hostel life - we unsurprisingly did not fare too well.

However, the experience gained through the sessions, working with components (which later helped during MS101 course and course project) and most importantly working with new people was a ton of fun. I reccomend every junior reading this to take part in this comp, no matter how daunting such a topic might be (it was for me initially).
